* **Subtitle:** An Offset-Well Intelligence and Drilling Decision-Support Layer for Oil India Limited

  * **Solution:** A proactive, depth-aware historical correlation engine that alerts drilling engineers before downhole hazards occur.

We present NWIS: Nearby Wells Intelligence System, an industrial intelligence layer designed to match historical offset experiences with active drilling operations in real time."*

### Slide 2: The Problem — Fragmented Drilling Knowledge
* **Slide Title:** The Problem: Trapped Knowledge & Blind Drilling Hazards
* **Visual Asset:** [`asset_2_problem_fragmented_knowledge.svg`](file:///d:/FINAL/NWISfinal/NWIS/presentation_assets/asset_2_problem_fragmented_knowledge.svg)
* **Slide Layout:** Place SVG centered or right-aligned; left side summarizes the 3 core pain points.
* **Key Bullet Points for Slide:**
  * **Isolated Data Silos:** Well completion reports (WCRs), daily drilling reports (DDRs), and mud logs exist in disconnected PDF files and paper folders.
  * **Manual Search Bottleneck:** Drilling engineers spend 12 to 18 hours manually scouring historical records per drilling section.
  * **Costly Downhole NPT:** Kicks, lost circulation, and stuck pipe incidents frequently recur in identical formations because offset lessons are not accessible in the drilling cabin.
* **Speaker Script / Talking Track:**
  > *"When an engineer encounters unexpected mud loss or high torque, locating what happened in an offset well drilled five years ago requires manual keyword searches across hundreds of PDF pages. By the time a report is found, the incident has already escalated into Non-Productive Time (NPT), costing between ₹25 Lakhs to over ₹1 Crore per day."*

* **Slide Title:** The NWIS Solution: Context-Aware Offset Intelligence
* **Visual Asset:** [`asset_3_solution_concept.svg`](file:///d:/FINAL/NWISfinal/NWIS/presentation_assets/asset_3_solution_concept.svg)
* **Slide Layout:** Full-width visual showing active well P-01 correlating with offset cluster X12, X09, and X21 within a 5.0 km corridor.

  * **Geospatial & Stratigraphic Proximity:** Dynamically identifies relevant nearby historical wells using geodesic radius filtering.
  * **Anticipatory Look-Ahead Buffer:** Projects 50m to 200m ahead of the active bit, surfacing past kicks, losses, and proven mitigations.

### Slide 4: System Architecture & Technical Pipeline
* **Slide Title:** System Architecture: From Unstructured Data to Rig Insights
* **Visual Asset:** [`asset_4_system_architecture.svg`](file:///d:/FINAL/NWISfinal/NWIS/presentation_assets/asset_4_system_architecture.svg)
* **Slide Layout:** Center the 6-layer architecture diagram. Emphasize the distinct separation between prototype reality and future integration.
* **Key Bullet Points for Slide:**
  * **Layer 1 (Data Sources):** Public NLOG records, historical DDR/WCR reports, and simulated telemetry for demonstration.
  * **Layer 2 (Processing):** Client-side Tesseract.js OCR, domain-specific rule-based regex extraction, and human validation.
  * **Layers 3 & 4 (Knowledge & Context):** Stratified relational wellbore models synchronized with active bit depth parameters.
  * **Layer 5 (Correlation Engine):** Multi-factor heuristic relevance scoring (proximity 40%, stratigraphy 30%, depth 15%, incident density 15%).
  * **Layer 6 (Decision Support):** Look-ahead hazard banners, evidence-linked Copilot Q&A, and printable shift intelligence briefs.
  * **Future Integration Pathway:** Designed with WITSML 1.4/2.0 data models for eventual live eRTMAC streaming.
* **Speaker Script / Talking Track:**
  > *"Our architecture is lightweight, air-gapped, and deterministic. Unstructured drilling reports undergo in-browser OCR and domain NLP extraction to create a structured knowledge base. Our correlation engine calculates a composite relevance score based on proximity, geological formation, and depth overlap. Crucially, every single recommendation cites the exact source report and paragraph, guaranteeing zero algorithmic hallucination."*

## 3. Hackathon Defense & Jury Technical Q&A Guide

When presenting to petroleum engineers, software evaluators, and OIL executives, use the following verified technical guidelines:

| Jury Question | Recommended Honest Technical Answer | What NOT to Say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Are you running an advanced deep learning / neural network model for hazard prediction?"** | *"In this prototype stage, we deliberately use a deterministic, domain-weighted heuristic scoring algorithm (proximity, formation, depth, and incident density). In high-stakes drilling, explainability and traceability are critical. Every recommendation links directly to verified historical DDR documents. Machine learning models represent a planned future enhancement once validated training datasets are established."* | ❌ Do not claim you built a trained neural network that autonomously predicts kicks with 99% accuracy. |
| **"Is this currently connected to live telemetry from an Oil India rig?"** | *"No. The prototype utilizes simulated telemetry and historical NLOG datasets to demonstrate end-to-end functionality. We have designed the data ingestion schema to be WITSML 1.4/2.0 compatible, representing a future integration pathway with OIL's eRTMAC ecosystem."* | ❌ Do not claim you are currently streaming live data from active OIL rigs in Duliajan. |
| **"Does NWIS take over or automate drilling parameters?"** | *"Absolutely not. NWIS is strictly an engineering decision-support and look-ahead advisory system. Full operational authority remains with the Drilling Superintendent and Company Representative. NWIS enhances crew situational awareness by answering 'what happened nearby at this depth?'"* | ❌ Do not claim the software autonomously steers drill strings or adjusts top-drive RPMs. |
| **"How do you handle unstandardized daily drilling reports (DDRs)?"** | *"We employ a client-side Tesseract OCR pipeline combined with domain-specific regex and keyword taxonomy to extract downhole events (loss, kick, tight hole, pack-off). In Stage 2, extracted events are flagged for human review by a data manager before entering the active knowledge base."* | ❌ Do not claim OCR has 100% automated accuracy without human verification. |
| **"Are these actual Oil India wells and real geological formations?"** | *"The prototype dataset utilizes public borehole records and anonymized synthetic wells (X03 to X21) with conceptual formations (F1 to F4) to demonstrate technical feasibility without exposing proprietary or confidential operator data."* | ❌ Do not claim these are confidential Oil India incident files or validated subsurface seismic models. |

## 4. How to Import and Use These Assets in PowerPoint

   * Modern versions of Microsoft PowerPoint (Office 365, PowerPoint 2019+) natively support SVG files. Simply drag any `.svg` file directly from `d:/FINAL/NWISfinal/NWIS/presentation_assets/` onto your presentation slide.
